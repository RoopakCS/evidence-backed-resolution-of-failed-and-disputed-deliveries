## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-trust, institutional crypto exchange and settlement interface. It departs from erratic web3 cliches—such as high-saturation neon washes, chaotic glow fields, and playful pill-shaped geometry—in favor of structural discipline, calm confidence, and clinical clarity.

### Design Movement: Institutional Modernism
The visual language merges Swiss grid discipline with contemporary financial precision:
- **Pristine Canvas Architecture:** Absolute whites (`#FFFFFF`) grounded by a deliberate near-black ink (`#101114`), giving every data point, chart, and transaction row immediate legibility.
- **Sculpted Purple Chromatics:** Docket Purple (`#5741D8`) operates as an authoritative anchor rather than decorative noise. It signals transactional intent, state shifts, and primary execution paths.
- **Architectonic Edge Profiles:** Strict 12px structural radiuses on core controls enforce tactile solidity. Full pill radiuses are explicitly forbidden on primary triggers to prevent a casual consumer feel.
- **Whisper-Depth Stratification:** Elevation is expressed through barely discernible ambient diffusion (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.03)`) and hairline architectural dividers (`#DEDEE5`) rather than heavy drop shadows.

## Colors

The palette is tuned around a triad of calculated purples balanced against a cool slate-neutral foundation:

- **Primary (`#5741D8`):** Deep Docket Purple. Anchors core action boundaries, active states, outlines, and high-emphasis focus indicators.
- **Secondary (`#7132F5`):** Bright Docket Purple. Dedicated to dominant call-to-actions, hyperlinked execution flows, and key conversion paths.
- **Tertiary (`#5B1ECF`):** Deepest Docket Purple. Used for pressed states, active navigation selections, and high-density transactional headers.
- **Neutral (`#686B82`):** Cool Slate Gray. Directs secondary content, table captions, and metadata indicators, providing contrast against bright background planes without creating visual vibration.

### Surface and Inking Rules
- Surface canvas must remain `#FFFFFF`.
- Text default ink is Near Black (`#101114`) for maximum legibility and strict contrast ratios. Never substitute pure black `#000000`.
- Dividers and structural partitions use Hairline Border Gray (`#DEDEE5`) at 1px thickness.
- Positive gains and settlement states use Success Green (`#149E61`) with a contextual container tint (`rgba(20, 158, 97, 0.16)`) and text rendered in Success Dark (`#026B3F`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the authoritative, geometric presence of **Manrope** for display elements with the ultra-clean, clinical legibility of **Hanken Grotesk** across UI surfaces.

- **Display & Section Titles (`Manrope`):** Set at weight 700 with deliberate negative tracking (`-0.5px` to `-1px`) to eliminate visual slack, projecting the gravitas of a tier-one financial desk.
- **Product UI & Ledger Body (`Hanken Grotesk`):** Built for numerical density, complex routing panels, and transactional verification tables. Standard tracking ensures clear glyph separation when rendering high-frequency digits, ticker symbols, and hash addresses.
- **Micro Badge & Precision Markers:** Set in bold caps with open tracking (`0.5px`) to deliver crisp visibility on fractional fee tags, protocol state chips, and execution status indicators.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout geometry follows an 8pt base grid with an intentional 4pt sub-scale for micro-alignment inside inputs and compact badge controls.

### Grid Engine
- **Desktop (1280px - 1536px+):** 12-column fluid grid, `2rem` (32px) gutters, and a maximum content containment width of 1440px. Section margins expand to `margin-desktop` (48px).
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and `1.5rem` outer margins.
- **Mobile (375px - 640px):** 4-column flow with `1rem` (16px) gutters and `1rem` outer canvas padding.

### Rhythm Principles
- Component inner paddings are strictly pegged: `space-sm` (8px) for compact tags, `space-md` (16px) for standard field buffers, and `space-lg` (24px) for card containers.
- Information panels preserve clean visual breaks through vertical stacks separated by multiples of 8px (e.g., 16px, 24px, 40px), avoiding erratic 1px/3px visual jitter in production templates.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy, atmospheric drop shadows and noisy blurs. Depth is conveyed strictly through architectural hairline borders paired with "whisper-level" ambient shadows.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** `#FFFFFF` surface resting on standard baseline without shadow. Boundary definition relies exclusively on 1px borders of `#DEDEE5`.
- **Level 1 (Whisper Ambient / Cards & Panels):** 
  - `box-shadow: 0px 4px 24px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.03);`
  - Border: `1px solid #DEDEE5`
  - Purpose: Primary application cards, metric widgets, and transaction breakdown containers.
- **Level 2 (Micro Elevation / Interactive Surfaces):**
  - `box-shadow: 0px 1px 4px rgba(16, 24, 40, 0.04);`
  - Border: `1px solid #DEDEE5`
  - Purpose: Unselected secondary buttons, active tab segment controls, and dropdown trigger bars.
- **Level 3 (Floating Menus & Modals):**
  - `box-shadow: 0px 12px 32px rgba(16, 24, 40, 0.08), 0px 2px 6px rgba(16, 24, 40, 0.04);`
  - Border: `1px solid #DEDEE5`
  - Purpose: Institutional order-routing dialogs, context menus, and asset pickers.

## Shapes

The design uses a controlled roundedness profile (`roundedness: 2`) governed by a strict architectural constraint:

- **12px Radius Core Rule:** All standard triggers, buttons, input fields, and action surfaces must maintain a consistent 12px corner radius.
- **No Pill Buttons:** Full pill geometry (`border-radius: 9999px`) is banned on primary and secondary buttons. Pill shapes soften the professional tone, which clashes with this system's focus on secure, institutional exchange operations.
- **Badge and Chip Radiuses:** Badges and metric status indicators use 6px or 8px radius to retain high data density without feeling rounded or soft.
- **Cards & Data Modules:** Enclosing cards and dialog windows utilize 16px radius (`rounded-lg`), creating a crisp hierarchy around internal 12px controls.

- **Text & Placeholder:** Typography `body-default`. Ink `#101114`; placeholder text `#9497A9`.
- **Focus State:** 1px solid `#5741D8` with an ambient glow outline: `0 0 0 3px rgba(87, 65, 216, 0.15)`.

### Badges & Status Chips
- **Success / Positive Delta:** 
  - Background: `rgba(20, 158, 97, 0.16)` | Text: `#026B3F`

- **Neutral / Meta Tag:** 
  - Background: `rgba(104, 107, 130, 0.12)` | Text: `#484B5E`
  - Border-radius: `8px` | Padding: `4px 10px` | Typography: `small-mono-ui`

### Cards & Module Panels
- **Structure:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #DEDEE5`, border-radius `16px`.
- **Elevation:** Whisper shadow `0px 4px 24px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.03)`.
- **Padding:** Internal content pad of `24px` (`space-lg`), separating header rows from data grids with `1px solid #DEDEE5` lines.

### Lists & Ledger Tables
- **Row Heights:** Minimum 52px for high-density ledgers; 64px for order execution history.
- **Separators:** Bottom borders using 1px solid `#DEDEE5`.
- **States:** Hover background tint `rgba(148, 151, 169, 0.04)`. Selected row border-left `3px solid #5741D8`.

### Checkboxes & Segment Controls
- **Checkboxes:** 20px x 20px, 6px border-radius, 1.5px border `#DEDEE5`. Active state: background `#5741D8` with white glyph check.
- **Segmented Switchers:** Outer container `#F7F7F9` with 10px radius, inner active tab `#FFFFFF` with 8px radius, whisper shadow `0px 1px 4px rgba(16, 24, 40, 0.04)`, and text `#101114`.
